Our story

🎨 Her style

Some days her garden is fancy: bright blooms, painted pots and flowers in full colour. Other days it is calm and minimal, with simple green leaves and room to breathe. Both moods are hers, and both feel like home.

👩‍🏫 Her work

She is a teacher at a government school, and she truly loves to teach. Beyond the classroom, she tends her plants with her own hands, from rooftop flowers to vegetables in recycled crates, and she cooks delicious food for her family. Teaching, growing and cooking: three ways she cares for the people and the world around her.

🚀 Ambition

To stay healthy and to live well. Every plant here grows toward that: fresh greens for the table, cleaner air to breathe, and quiet mornings that keep the heart light.
